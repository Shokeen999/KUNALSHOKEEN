Professional Summary

I am a Quality Audit and Customer Experience professional with 7+ years of experience across Quality Assurance, Customer Service, Sales Quality, Training, Operations and Process Excellence within customer-centric business environments.

Currently, as an Assistant Manager – Quality at Lenskart, I lead the entire Quality Audit and Compliance function for the Assisted Sales LOB, ensuring that customer interactions, sales processes and operational practices meet defined quality, compliance and customer experience standards.

Alongside Assisted Sales Quality, I also look after Customer Happiness Quality, focusing on customer experience indicators, customer complaints, quality trends, recurring pain points, customer-impacting defects and opportunities to improve the overall customer journey.

My role also extends into Training and capability development, where I work closely with Operations, Quality and Training stakeholders to identify skill gaps, convert quality insights into targeted learning interventions, conduct calibration and feedback sessions, support refresher training and improve individual and team performance.

My approach combines quality governance, customer insights, data analysis, coaching, training and process improvement to create measurable improvements in customer experience and operational performance.
